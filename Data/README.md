# Financial Workflows
This repository is structured to reflect the complete accounting cycle. Below is a detailed explanation of each section and workflow:

1. **General-Journal**  
   The foundational data entry point. This section contains the chronological log of all daily financial transactions, detailing dates, account names, debits, and credits before they are categorized.

2. **General-Ledger**  
   The master repository of accounts. It categorizes all transactions from the General Journal into specific individual accounts to track their running balances over the accounting period.

3. **Adjusting-Entries**  
   The end-of-period refinements. This includes crucial journal entries made at the end of the month—such as machine depreciation and accrued expenses, to ensure the financial records comply with the accrual basis of accounting.

4. **Adjusted Trial-Balance**  
   The pre-reporting checkpoint. A summarized worksheet listing all finalized account balances after adjustments have been applied. It acts as a mathematical verification, ensuring that total debits perfectly match total credits before generating financial statements.

5. **Income-Statement**  
   The profitability tracker. This financial report details the company's operational performance, revealing the final Net Profit or Loss for the period.

6. **Balance-Sheet**  
   The snapshot of financial health. It reports the company's financial position, structurally balancing Assets against Liabilities and Equity.

7. **Cashflow-Statement**  
   The liquidity monitor. It breaks down actual cash inflows and outflows, categorizing cash movements into Operating, Investing, and Financing activities to show exactly how cash was generated and utilized.

8. **Statement-of-Changes-in-Equity**  
   The capital movement log. This report tracks all fluctuations in the owners' equity over the period, highlighting initial capital injections, additional paid-in investments, and accumulated retained earnings.

9. **Closing-Entries**  
   The period-end reset. Contains the final journal entries required to zero out temporary accounts (revenues and expenses) and transfer their balances into permanent equity accounts, preparing the books for the next accounting cycle.
